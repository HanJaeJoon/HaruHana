# English (en-US)

## 앱 이름 (30자)

HaruHana

## 간단한 설명 (80자)

One goal this year, one thing today. No streaks, no scores, no colors.

## 자세한 설명 (4,000자)

HaruHana holds one goal for the year and one daily action that moves it forward. Nothing else.

Habit apps fill up. Drink water, stretch, review flashcards. Keeping ten small promises quietly pushes aside the one thing you meant to do this year. HaruHana cannot hold a list. One goal, one thing today.

How it works
- Write one goal for this year (for example: reach my target test score)
- Write the one thing that moves it forward (for example: solve problems for at least an hour)
- Each day, mark whether you did it

No streak counter
Most habit apps show "12 days in a row" and reset it to zero when you miss once. Protecting that number becomes the point, and the goal fades. HaruHana counts total days done. A missed day does not undo anything; it was simply a day without progress.

66 days
A number often cited for how long a habit takes to settle. HaruHana treats it as a scale mark, not a target. When your total reaches 66 it says so once, and after that it shows the running total and this month's density.

No color
The screen is black, white, and grey. No red warnings, no green rewards. Steering someone with color is itself a game mechanic. Days you did it are a filled dot; everything else stays blank.

The app does not judge
There is no timer and no counter. "At least an hour" lives only in the wording you wrote, and whether you did it today is your call. The app just records the answer.

Today and yesterday only
Backfilling last week is not a record. Only today and yesterday can be edited. If yesterday is blank, the app asks about it once the next time you open it.

Features
- Mark the one thing done or not done
- Total days done and progress toward 66
- This month's density (for example: 18/22 this month)
- A calendar that marks only the days you did it
- An archive that keeps finished goals with their records
- A daily reminder at a time you choose, or none
- Dark mode
- Korean and English

Works without an internet connection
No account, no sign-in. Your goal and records stay on the device and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them too, so write down anything you want to keep.

This app shows ads. There is a single banner at the bottom of the screen - no interstitials and no rewarded ads.
