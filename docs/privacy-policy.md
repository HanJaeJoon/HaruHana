This policy explains what HaruHana (the "app") does and does not collect. It applies to the Android app published by Jaejoon Han under the package name `com.hanjaejoon.haruhana`.

## What the app does not collect

The app has no account, no login and no server of its own.

Your goal, your daily one thing, your daily check marks and your reminder time are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. **They are never transmitted, and the developer never receives them.** Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner ad through **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID
- IP address, device model, operating system version, and coarse (country or region level) location derived from those
- ad interactions such as impressions and clicks

The developer does not receive your advertising ID or any personally identifying data from this. Google acts as an independent data controller for the data it collects through ads.

Google's handling of this data is described in the **Google Privacy Policy** (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and **How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services** (<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in **Settings > Google > Ads** on your Android device. The same screen lets you opt out of ad personalisation.

## Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet access | Required to load the banner ad. Everything else in the app works offline |
| Notifications | Only to show the daily reminder you scheduled. The reminder is created and delivered entirely on your device; no push server is involved |

Declining the notification permission only disables the reminder. The rest of the app is unaffected, and you can turn the reminder off at any time in the app's settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Data retention and deletion

The developer stores no data about you, so there is nothing to request deletion of. Your goal and records live only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (**Settings > Apps > HaruHana > Storage > Clear data**).

For data collected by Google through ads, see Google's policy linked above.

## Not medical or psychological advice

The app is a personal record-keeping tool. The figure of 66 days is a commonly cited reference for how long a habit takes to settle; the app uses it as a scale mark and makes no claim about any individual outcome. The app does not measure, verify or diagnose anything - you decide whether you did your one thing.
